# Modular Glide System

## Tactical Advanced Range Augmentation (TARA)

[The Tactical Advanced Range Augmentation (TARA)](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2258934&reg=3&lang=2) is India's first domestic modular glide weapon kit designed by the Research Centre Imarat (RCI) to convert standard unguided "dumb bombs" into high-precision, long-range standoff munitions.

- TARA is India’s first indigenous glide weapon system that converts ordinary unguided air-dropped bombs into precision-guided weapons.
- Aim: To enhance the lethality and accuracy of a low-cost weapon to neutralise ground-based targets.
- It has been designed and developed by the Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad, along with other DRDO laboratories.

### How does TARA work?

- Once an aircraft releases a TARA-fitted munition, the system deploys folding wings and aerodynamic surfaces to generate lift, enabling the weapon to glide well beyond normal free-fall range.
- Onboard GPS/INS-based guidance steers it precisely to a ground-based target. Thus, instead of falling directly like a conventional bomb, it can:
- Travel much farther and strike with greater precision.
- Allow aircraft to attack targets from safer stand-off distances.

### Advantages of glide bombs

- Keeps aircraft & pilot away from enemy defences
- Glides at high subsonic speeds, difficult to intercept
- Cheaper than missiles; no complex propulsion systems
- Utilises existing stockpiles of unguided bombs
- Enhances precision strike capability at lower cost
- Circular error probable (CEP) often within 10 meters

### Why is TARA significant for India?

- Boosts Indigenous defence capability: TARA represents a major step toward defence self-reliance and Indigenous military modernisation.
- Cost-Effective Precision Warfare: Instead of manufacturing expensive missiles, India can upgrade existing ordinary unguided bombs into smart weapons. This improves strike accuracy at a lower cost.
- Enhanced Stand-Off Capability: Aircraft can release the weapon from safer distances, reducing vulnerability to Surface-to-air missiles and enemy interception.

### Key Specifications

- Type: Modular range-extension glide kit for conventional unguided bombs.
- Developer: Research Centre Imarat (RCI) (DRDO) along with Indian industry partners like Adani Defence & Aerospace.
- Compatible Weight Configurations: 250 kg, 450 kg, and 500 kg bomb classes.
- Operational Range: 150 km to 180 km when released from an altitude of 5 km (approx. 16,000 ft) at speeds around Mach 0.8.
- Accuracy: Less than 3 meters Circular Error Probable (CEP) using advanced terminal seekers.
- Guidance System:
  - Mid-course: Fiber Optic Gyroscope Inertial Navigation System (FOG-INS) + Multi-GNSS/GPS.T
  - erminal: Electro-Optical (EO) / Imaging Infrared (IIR) homing.
- Compatible Launch Platforms: SEPECAT Jaguar, Dassault Mirage 2000, Sukhoi Su-30MKI, and HAL Tejas.
