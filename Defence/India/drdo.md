# Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)

## About DRDO

DRDO stands for the Defence Research and Development Organisation.

It is the premier agency under the Ministry of Defence in India. It handles military research and development to make the country self-reliant in defense technologies.

### Key Facts

- Established: 1958Headquarters: New Delhi, India
- Motto: "Strength's Origin is in Science" (Balasya Mulam Vigyanam)
- Parent Agency: Department of Defence Research and Development, Government of India

### What DRDO Does

- Designs and develops advanced weapons, missiles, and equipment for the Indian Armed Forces.
- Operates a network of over 50 laboratories across the country.
- Works on projects involving aeronautics, combat vehicles, electronics, and naval systems.
- Famous projects include the Agni and Prithvi missile series, the Tejas light combat aircraft, and the Pinaka rocket launcher.

## RCI

Research Centre Imarat (RCI) is a premier DRDO laboratory located in Hyderabad, Telangana, that designs and develops advanced avionics, missile systems, and guided weapons.

### Key Facts

- Established: 1988 by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
- Location: Vignyanakancha, Hyderabad, Telangana
- Parent Body: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Missile Complex, Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)
- Core Focus: Avionics, inertial navigation, control engineering, RF and imaging infrared seekers, and onboard computers
